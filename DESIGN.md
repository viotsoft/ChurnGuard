# Design System — ChurnGuard

> Generated by /design-consultation on 2026-05-26  
> Branch: greenfield (pre-code)

---

## Product Context

- **What this is:** AI-powered churn prediction + automated retention execution for e-commerce shop owners. Owner gets an approval notification ("Maria is at risk — send birthday discount?"), approves with one tap, ChurnGuard executes via Klaviyo.
- **Who it's for:** Solo e-commerce shop owners. Shopify/WooCommerce. €100K–€2M annual revenue. 1–3 person teams. Time-poor. No dedicated CS team.
- **Space/industry:** E-commerce retention SaaS, European market. Peers: Klaviyo Predictive Analytics, Chupper, EigenAI, Gainsight (enterprise), Churn Buster.
- **Project type:** Web app (approval queue + dashboard) + email-driven approval flow

---

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Direction:** Editorial Utility
- **Decoration level:** Intentional — warmth through typography and warm surface colors, not illustrative elements. No gradients, no blobs, no decorative patterns.
- **Mood:** The feeling of a very smart bookkeeper who uses beautiful notebooks. Serious but not corporate. Warm but not playful. Decisive without being alarming. Approving a customer action should feel as low-stakes and reassuring as signing a receipt.
- **North star:** "Finally, a tool that does it for you." Every design decision serves this. Execution over analysis. Actions over dashboards.
- **Reference sites:** Researched Klaviyo, Gainsight, Churn Buster, RetentionX (2026-05-26). Deliberately diverged from all of them.

**EUREKA (logged):** Every competitor in this category designs for analytics *viewers* — dashboards, charts, risk scores, trend lines. ChurnGuard's core interaction is an *approval*. The product is closer to a task manager or push notification than a BI dashboard. Design it accordingly.

## Typography

- **Display / Hero / Customer names:** [Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces) — Weighted humanist serif. "Maria Kowalski" in Fraunces 32px feels like a person, not a database row. Immediately distinctive in a category where everyone uses geometric sans. Load via Google Fonts (`opsz,wght@9..144,400;9..144,600;9..144,700`).
- **UI / Navigation / Labels / Body:** [Instrument Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Sans) — Crisp, slightly compressed, functional. Works at every size from 11px labels to 20px subheadings.
- **Data / Revenue figures / Tables:** [Geist](https://vercel.com/font) or [Geist Mono](https://vercel.com/font) — Use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on all revenue and percentage figures. Monospace ensures columns align precisely.
- **Code / Customer IDs / API keys:** [Berkeley Mono](https://berkeleygraphics.com/typefaces/berkeley-mono/) — Fallback: `JetBrains Mono`, then `monospace`.
- **Loading strategy:** Google Fonts for Fraunces + Instrument Sans (`display=swap`). Geist from Vercel CDN or self-host. Berkeley Mono self-host (commercial license required).

## Layout

- **Approach:** Hybrid — action-first for the approval queue (portrait-oriented centered cards), grid-disciplined for data tables and settings.
- **Primary screen (approval queue):** No left sidebar. Minimal header only: logo left | "12 pending" pill center | settings icon right. Cards centered, max-width 560px. Portrait-oriented even on desktop — designed to be approved from a phone.
- **Secondary screens (customer list, insights, settings):** Standard left-nav + content area layout. Left nav appears only here — not on the primary approval screen.
- **Max content width:** 1100px (marketing), 560px (approval queue cards), 900px (data tables)
- **Grid:** 12-column at desktop (≥1024px), 4-column at tablet (768–1023px), 1-column at mobile
- **Border radius:**
  - `--radius-sm: 4px` — badges, small chips
  - `--radius-md: 8px` — inputs, small buttons
  - `--radius-lg: 12px` — alerts, medium components
  - `--radius-xl: 16px` — cards, modals, app frame
  - `--radius-full: 9999px` — pills, avatars, toggles

**DESIGN RULE:** Charts live in the Insights section (3 taps deep). They never appear on the primary approval queue screen. The home screen metric is "N pending approvals" — not a retention curve.

## Motion

- **Approach:** Minimal-functional — only transitions that aid comprehension or confirm action.
- **Easing:**
  - Enter: `cubic-bezier(0.0, 0.0, 0.2, 1)` (ease-out — elements arrive quickly, decelerate)
  - Exit: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.0, 1, 1)` (ease-in — elements leave quickly)
  - Move: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.0, 0.2, 1)` (ease-in-out — symmetric repositioning)
- **Duration:**
  - Micro: 50–100ms (hover states, focus rings)
  - Short: 150–250ms (button press, badge appearance)
  - Medium: 250–400ms (card flip on approval, modal open/close)
  - Long: 400–700ms (page transitions, skeleton loading)
- **Signature motion:** Approval card flip (CSS 3D `rotateY`, 300ms medium ease-in-out). Card flips to show confirmation state: "Discount sent to Maria · Est. recovery €89". Then slides down into "Done" section. The flip signals completion; the slide signals it's recorded.
- **No decorative animation.** No scroll-triggered entrance animations, no bouncing loaders, no lottie illustrations.

## Components

### Approval Card
The product's primary UI element. Fixed layout:
1. Customer name (Fraunces 28–32px, top-left)
2. Revenue at risk (Geist Mono, terracotta, top-right) — shown BEFORE context. Answers "is this worth 3 seconds?"
3. Customer metadata (Instrument Sans 13px, muted)
4. Risk badge (traffic-light semantic)
5. Risk reason (italic, surface-2 background, left accent border in `--accent-light`)
6. Proposed action label (11px uppercase, muted)
7. Full-width primary CTA button (terracotta)
8. Full-width ghost "Skip for now" button below

### Risk Badges
Three tiers, always traffic-light:
- High: `--danger-light` background, `--danger` text + dot
- Medium: `--warning-light` background, `--warning` text + dot
- Low: `--success-light` background, `--success` text + dot

### Buttons
- Primary: `--accent` fill, white text, `--radius-md`
- Secondary: transparent, `--border` border, `--text`
- Ghost: no border, `--text-muted`
- All: Instrument Sans 14px 600, 10px/20px padding, transition 150ms

## Integrations Visual Language

When showing Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Shopify, or Twilio integration status:
- Use their official brand colors/logos
- Show connection status as a small badge (connected = `--success`, disconnected = `--danger`)
- Never hide integration logos — they're trust signals for the target audience

---

## Anti-patterns (never do these)

- ❌ Purple/violet gradient as accent or hero background
- ❌ 3-column feature grid with icons in colored circles
- ❌ Centered everything with uniform spacing
- ❌ Uniform bubbly border-radius (≥16px) on ALL elements — use the radius scale
- ❌ Gradient buttons
- ❌ Charts on the primary/home screen
- ❌ Inter, Roboto, Space Grotesk, or system-ui as display font
- ❌ Dark navy / deep blue color scheme (that's Gainsight — not ChurnGuard)
- ❌ "Built for shop owners" / "Designed for e-commerce" marketing copy patterns
